Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the restoration take?

Most restorations are completed within 24 to 48 hours. Severe damage may take up to 72 hours for meticulous manual repair.

What if I am not satisfied with the result?

We offer unlimited revisions. We will work with you until you are completely satisfied with the restored photo.

Can extremely blurry photos be fixed?

Yes, we use advanced AI upscaling and facial enhancement algorithms to restore lost details in blurry photographs.
